Import & export

Take your notes out of InkNote, or bring notes in. It works with the notes on this device, also without a connection.

Export

All your notes as one zip file of Markdown files: a folder for each notebook, a header with the title, tags, status and pin, and the pictures and files of your notes next to them. Any Markdown tool can open it, and InkNote can read it back. Notes in the trash are not included. To export a single note, use "Export" in its menu in the sidebar.

Import

Choose Markdown files (.md, .markdown, .txt) or zip files of them: one made by InkNote, or from another app. A folder of a zip becomes a notebook, the tags in a file's header become tags, and pictures and PDFs that a note links to are attached to it. Nothing you have is changed: importing the same file twice makes copies.
